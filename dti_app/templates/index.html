How to Read and Interpret Binding Predictions

Δ pAffinity Log Scale

pAffinity = 9 − log10(nM)

Every 1.0 unit change represents a 10-fold difference in drug concentration. Higher pAffinity indicates tighter binding at lower concentrations.

Δ Ki vs IC50 Tasks

pKi measures true thermodynamic equilibrium dissociation constant.
pIC50 measures 50% functional inhibition in an assay. Both are trained separately.

Δ Cold-Drug Scaffold Honesty

Predictions use Bemis-Murcko scaffold cold-drug models as primary estimates. Typical expected test RMSE is ~0.98–1.05 log units for novel chemotypes.

Strong (≥ 8.0) ≤ 10 nM (Drug-like lead potency)
Moderate (6.0 – 8.0) 10 nM – 1 µM (Hit-to-lead range)
Weak (< 6.0) > 1 µM (Low/negligible specific binding)
