What This Function Means

Physics-based conformational search to evaluate steric fit and hydrogen bonding in the 3D binding pocket.

Required Inputs

1. 4-letter PDB ID (e.g. 1M17).
2. Co-crystal ligand (defines pocket centroid).
3. Query SMILES (leave empty to redock crystal ligand).

Conclusion & Physics Score

Returns Vina affinity score in kcal/mol. Negative values indicate favorable binding free energy (ΔG).
