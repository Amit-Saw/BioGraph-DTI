Model Architecture, Benchmarks & Limitations

Complete transparency on neural network topologies, experimental training sets, Bemis-Murcko cold-scaffold generalization, and biological caveats.

Neural Network Topology

Two-tower late-fusion network: GNN drug graph encoder (128-d) concatenated with frozen ESM-2 (8M) protein embedding (128-d).

Scaffold Generalization

Trained and tested with cold_drug splits to evaluate genuine prospective generalization on unencountered chemotypes.

Expected Prediction Error

Empirical test RMSE on novel scaffolds is ~0.98–1.05 log units (~10-fold concentration factor).

Two-Tower Late-Fusion Architecture Blueprint

SMILES String ──► Mol Graph (38-d Atom, 7-d Bond) ──► [GNN Encoder: AttentiveFP / MPNN] ──► 128-d Drug Vector ──┐ ├─► Concat (256-d) ──► MLP Head ──► pAffinity Amino Sequence ──► ESM-2 (8M Frozen Embeddings) ──► 320-d Vector ──► [MLP 320→256→128] ──► 128-d Prot Vector ─┘
Ligand Graph Representation

Atom features (38-d) encode atomic numbers, degree, formal charge, hybridization, aromaticity, and ring membership. Bond features (7-d) encode single/double/triple/aromatic types, conjugation, and rings.

ESM-2 Language Model

facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D evaluates long sequences using sliding windows (window=900, stride=800), mean-pooling hidden residue states into one 320-dimensional evolutionary representation.

GNN Graph Encoders

AttentiveFP applies atom-level and bond-level attention mechanisms; MPNN utilizes edge-conditioned NNConv message passing with GRU updates and Set2Set readout.

Known Scientific Limitations & Caveats

  • Uncertainty Scale on New Scaffolds: For molecules distinct from the training set, typical error is approximately ±1.0 log unit (a factor of ten in nM). Always consult the uncertainty range bar rather than relying on a point estimate.
  • Coarse Protein Representation: The protein is encoded as an evolutionary sequence vector from ESM-2; it does not explicitly represent 3D pocket geometry, conformational flexibility, or induced-fit changes.
  • Assay & Laboratory Label Noise: BindingDB aggregates measurements across thousands of research laboratories and diverse experimental assay protocols. This inter-laboratory variance establishes an empirical noise floor of ~0.5–0.7 log units.
  • Chemical Domain: Models are trained on small drug-like organic molecules (H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br, I). Large peptides, biologics, polymers, and metal complexes are outside the validity domain.
  • Regulatory Disclaimer: Predictions are statistical research estimates intended to guide exploratory hypothesis generation and compound prioritization; they are not certified for clinical or regulatory diagnostic decisions.
