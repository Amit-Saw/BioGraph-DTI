Batch Chemical Library Scoring

Rapidly score thousands of small molecules against a single target protein. Processes molecules in parallel through the graph neural network, computes binding affinity metrics, and produces a structured, exportable dataset.

What This Function Means

High-throughput batch evaluation to prioritize drug-like molecules from internal libraries or vendor catalogs.

Required Inputs

A CSV/TSV with a smiles column (and optional name column), or text file with 1 SMILES per line (up to 5,000 rows).

Conclusion & Output

Sortable affinity table with calculated concentrations (nM) and direct one-click CSV export.
